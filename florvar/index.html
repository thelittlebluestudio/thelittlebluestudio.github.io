How do I play?

Tap to jump. Tap again in the air for one extra flap. Stay on the ground to go under branches and birds. Munch every snack you can on the way. There's a full guide under "How to play" on the game's home screen.

How do I open new cities?

Finish a city and collect enough snacks in total to open the next one. Edinburgh, Manchester, Glasgow and London are free. The full game opens the other cities and the Seasons, Wonders and Space packs.

I bought the full game. How do I get it on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the game, tap Play, then the locked Paris level, and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android) you bought it with. You never pay twice.

My progress has gone

Your snacks, stars and outfits are saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes them too, and they don't move to a new phone. The full game purchase itself is never lost: use Restore purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone and iPad, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play Store's order history.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version: Florvar's Snack Run does not collect any personal information. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What the game does with your information

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No data collection. We don't collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. We don't know who you are.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Snacks, stars, outfits and settings are saved only inside the game on your phone or tablet. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and it doesn't need the internet to play.

Leaderboards

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center (or Google Play Games on Android), your scores are sent to that service so they can appear on the leaderboards, along with the nickname you chose there. This is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We don't receive or keep it. If you'd rather not take part, sign out of Game Center or Play Games, and the game still works.

Purchases

The full game is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment and tell the game only whether the full game has been bought. We never see your name, card details or account.

Sharing

The Share button makes a picture of your score that you can send with any app you choose. The game doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

The game is made for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
